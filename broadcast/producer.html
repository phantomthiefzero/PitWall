Incidents

iRacing keeps other drivers' incident points hidden until the race ends, so these are predicted from what every car's telemetry does: going off, spinning, losing its speed, two cars losing it together, tows and damage flags. The likely points follow iRacing's table. When the official counts appear after the finish, they are compared here car by car.

How to set it up

Your co-host spectates the same session in their own iRacing. They open vdo.ninja, choose to share their screen (the iRacing window), and send you the view link it gives; paste it above, then switch Picture in picture on under On air. The video plays inside the overlay, in a framed window you can move and resize under Look, Position.

If your co-host runs the suite too, they paste your co-host link into Second camera on their home page. Then the caption follows their camera by itself, and picking a car or a shot here moves their camera (only while your broadcast is on).

Starting grid

Automatic: when the pace laps start, the camera goes down the grid in order, a row at a time (both cars on screen, the camera on each in turn) or one car at a time, with the grid beside it. It fits the walk into the pace laps: shorter holds when time is short, and if even that will not fit, the front rows and each class's pole. The full grid board shows before and after the walk. Both come off at the green.

Walk the grid

The grid board first

Every car on one screen, with its picture, a light running down the grid while the camera stays wide; then the row cards. Automatic: at a standing start (no pace laps, cars still arriving) and when the pace laps are too short for every car.

Auto-director

With replays on, the director shows the best recent moment again (a contact, a spin, a pass for the top places, a new leader) when nothing big is happening live, or under a caution: with the stinger in and out, from another angle, and straight back to live if something big happens meanwhile. At most one every 75 s.

League standings

The league's own table, however you have it: a link to the league's standings page or a shared Google Sheet, a file (CSV, TSV, JSON or a saved web page), a table pasted from anywhere, or typed in below. Columns are found by their headings (position, driver or name, points, number, class, customer id); without headings, the text column is the name and the last number the points. Drivers are matched by customer id, else by name (iRacing's added digits and "Last, First" are fine), else by car number. The points are the league's: nothing is worked out here. They show on the League graphic and the grid walk.

Car pictures

Automatic: iRacing draws each car with its paint, Trading Paints included, the same way its own screens do. Nothing to set up; keep Trading Paints running so the paints are on this PC.

Turn this off to test whether iRacing closing by itself has to do with the pictures: the ones already taken are kept and still shown. They are asked for one at a time, and never while you are driving. Everything the app asks of iRacing is written to sim_log.txt in the app's picture folder (), the last lines before "sim gone" being what it was doing when the sim closed.

Photograph the field

This cuts the camera to each car in turn, so do it before going live, or during the grid walk. Photos are kept for next time.

Driver pictures

A photo for a driver goes on the driver card and the battle graphic, kept for every race by their iRacing ID. Without one, that space is left out (the card still shows their helmet). Ask drivers before putting their face on a stream.

Camera and replay check

Sends each camera and replay command the broadcast uses to iRacing once, and checks what iRacing did: two cuts, a camera change, iRacing's own choice of the leader, pause, slow motion, play, a replay of a moment 30 s ago, back to live, and the camera back where it was. About a minute. Run it while spectating a session with cars on track; it takes the camera from the director while it runs. Every step is written to sim_log.txt and the result to sim_check.json beside the car pictures.
