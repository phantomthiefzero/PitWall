Lap Analyzer

Look back at laps you have driven: your lap against a reference lap, corner by corner and channel by channel. Drop one of your iRacing .ibt files (Documents\iRacing\telemetry) on the left and, to compare, a Garage 61 lap (.csv) or another .ibt file on the right. Everything runs on this PC; nothing is uploaded.

Start here
  1. Drop your lap in the left box. A single .ibt file is enough: with no reference, it is compared with your own 2nd fastest lap.
  2. Drop a reference lap on the right to compare against. Use a Garage 61 lap (.csv), or a second .ibt file.
  3. An .ibt file also lists every lap in it. For what to work on in the driving, open Coaching; for the car, open Setup.
