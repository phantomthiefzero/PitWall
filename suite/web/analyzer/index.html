Lap Analyzer

Drop one of your own iRacing .ibt files (from Documents\iRacing\telemetry) to analyze your laps and car setup. Add a reference on the right, a Garage 61 CSV or another .ibt file, to compare against another lap or driver. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Start here
  1. Drop your lap in the left box. A single .ibt file is enough: with no reference, it is compared with your own fastest lap.
  2. Drop a reference lap on the right to compare against. Use a Garage61 CSV, or a second .ibt file.
  3. Your own-laps view appears when you load an .ibt file. Name the turns there to get labelled coaching.
