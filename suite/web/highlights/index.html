Race Highlights

Turns a whole race's replay into a short video of one car's best moments.

1 The race

Open the race's replay in iRacing (it should be playing), then press Check iRacing.

2 The moments

Target length minutes

Ticked moments go in the video. The start and the finish are always in. Moments close together become one clip.

Race timeLapWhatLengthScore

3 Look and sound

OBS

In OBS: Tools > WebSocket Server Settings > Enable WebSocket server (Show Connect Info gives the password). Your OBS scene should show iRacing (Game Capture), plus your broadcast overlay if you want it in the video.

Camera
Stinger

The built-in stingers sweep across in amber and dark and show your car number and team while the picture is covered. Your own: one with a transparent background (.webm, .mov or an animated .gif) plays over each cut; one without plays between the clips.

Captions Title and finish cards Music

4 What to make

Full video Shorts
  and one short each for the top moments

Vertical (9:16, 1080 x 1920) for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. Each opens straight into its best moment, then shows it again from other cameras, and ends without a fade so it plays round. One short line of text, inside the area the apps never cover, gone after 3 seconds. Twitch clips are cut from a live stream on Twitch itself, so they can't be made here; these files are for uploading.

Short length
 Up to 3 minutes is allowed, but shorts that people watch to the end do better: 15 to 30 seconds.

Replays

5 Make the videos

The app takes over the replay: it jumps to each clip, points the camera at your car and records it with OBS. A clip used by more than one video is recorded once. Leave iRacing and OBS alone until it finishes (about as long as the clips, plus a few seconds each).
